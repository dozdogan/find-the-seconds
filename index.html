⏱️ Find the Seconds

Watch the green light. Guess how long it was on.

How to play
  1. Choose how many players. A game has 3 rounds.
  2. Press Start. The green light turns on. Everyone counts in their head.
  3. After a random time (up to 10 seconds) the light switches back to red.
  4. Each player types their guess, like 4 . 37 for 4.37 seconds.
  5. The player with the lowest total time off after 3 rounds wins!
